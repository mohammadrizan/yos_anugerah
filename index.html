Workshop Mission & Executive Summary

In collaboration with the team from Koding Next Batam, I led and presented an interactive coding socialization workshop hosted at SMK Yos Anugerah Batam. Designed specifically for senior high school students across grades 10 to 12, the initiative aimed to bridge the gap between classroom learning and real-world tech careers.

Through engaging lectures, practical Python demonstrations, and career orientation sessions, we unpacked how learning modern programming languages opens up post-graduation opportunities in software development, data analysis, and digital technology fields.

Phase 01

Inspiring the Next Generation

We kicked off the workshop with a high-energy socialization session exploring why coding is becoming a foundational skill across industries globally.

  • Mapping out career options in Software Engineering, AI, and Data Science.
  • Discussing university and industry pathways after high school graduation.
  • Demystifying computer science for beginners.
Phase 02

Interactive Python Fundamentals

Students dove straight into writing their first lines of Python code. We focused on core syntax and logic building using approachable examples.

  • Understanding variables, data types, and simple arithmetic logic.
  • Constructing conditional logic (if/else) and repetition with loops.
  • Building mini interactive CLI programs on the spot.
Phase 03

Team Leadership & Interactive Q&A

Leading alongside my team members from Koding Next Batam, we conducted 1-on-1 mentoring circles around student desks to solve coding errors and answer personal career questions.

  • Direct guidance for students tackling programming challenges.
  • Interactive Q&A regarding skills needed for IT certifications.
  • Collaborative teamwork fostering an encouraging environment.

Impact & Key Outcomes

Key achievements resulting from the workshop collaboration between Koding Next Batam and SMK Yos Anugerah.

Career Pathways

Elevated awareness among Grade 10–12 students regarding high-demand technology roles and higher-education options post-graduation.

Python Fundamentals

Equipped students with practical understanding of variables, conditional logic, loops, and basic algorithmic problem solving.

Team Leadership

Successfully coordinated speaker presentations, live code demonstrations, and table-side team mentoring seamlessly.

School Collaboration

Strengthened ties between Koding Next Batam and SMK Yos Anugerah for future educational outreach programs.
